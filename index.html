Learn

One person taps every card that turns face up. The app keeps the count, your edge and the signal, and tells you how to play your hand. It is about one tap per card.

Before the first hand

  1. Match the rules.Open Settings and set how many decks you shuffle together, whether the dealer hits soft 17, and what blackjack pays. Two decks shuffled together means Decks: 2.
  2. Start on a fresh shuffle.Shuffle the real cards, open Cards, and press Shuffle twice. The count has to start from a full shoe; joining halfway gives a wrong count until the next shuffle.
  3. Choose when to sit in.In Settings, “Enter at +1” and “Leave at −1” is a good start. Higher entry means fewer hands, each one better.

Every round

  1. Look at the signal before the deal.WAIT or LEAVE: don’t bet. ENTER or PLAY: bet the amount shown. Tick “I’m playing now” in the signal box when you sit in.
  2. Tap the other players’ cards.Choose Others, then tap each face-up card they get.

  3. Tap the dealer’s up card.Choose Dealer and tap it. The app switches to Me by itself.

  5. Tap every extra card.Your hits go under Me. Everyone else’s hits, the dealer’s hidden card when it is turned over, and the dealer’s extra cards go under Others.
  6. Press the orange button.It starts the next round. The count carries on until you shuffle.

Good to know

  • 10, J, Q and K all use the 10 button. They count the same.
  • Sitting out? Keep tapping every card under Others. The count needs every card your friends get.
  • Burned or face-down cards that nobody sees: don’t tap them.
  • Wrong tap? Press Undo. If a rank runs out, the app refuses it, which catches miscounts.
  • Reshuffled? Press Shuffle twice. The count resets to 0.
  • On a laptop the keys are faster: 2–9, 0 for any ten, A for aces, O / D / P to choose Others / Dealer / Me, Backspace to undo, N for the next round.

What the signal means

WAITThe count is below your entry level. Watch and keep tapping.
ENTERThe count reached your entry level. Sit down with the bet shown.
PLAYYou’re in. Keep playing at the bet shown, even through small dips.
LEAVEThe count fell to your exit level. Sit out until it recovers or the cards are reshuffled.

Practise first

Use the Drill tab until you can count a deck in under 25 seconds, and play a few shoes on the Table screen, where the app deals and you can check your count with “Hide count” turned on.

Every Hi-Lo index play for these rules. I18 and Fab 4 are the published indexes worth the most. Computed ones are worked out by this app: it builds the shoe you’d have at each true count and finds the count where another play becomes better. Checked against the published Illustrious 18, it lands within one count of every one. “≥ +3” means make the play at true count +3 or higher; “below −2” means only when the count is under −2.

BC = betting correlation, PE = playing efficiency, IC = insurance correlation (1.00 = perfect). Most professionals use Hi-Lo or KO.
